# Events

Events would happen throughout the game, which players are going to make
decisions to continue the event, lastly may give bonuses or punishments
regarding what they chose.

The events are mostly based on real world past events, with a bit of
adjustments. 事件发生时时间会暂停，直到确认后才继续。

按持续事件与瞬时事件划分。
