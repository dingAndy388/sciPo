===== Game Index 指標 =====

There is multiple types of index in the game to effect the development of civilisation.
<note important>该页内容已被废弃</note>

==== Prosperity 繁榮度 ====

The Index of game progression, calculated by manpower, amount of resource, buildings, technologies discovered.

[Formula]

  * One of the trigger requirements of events
  * Different levels of prosperity allows/disables events happening, chances of finding specialists, effects of buffs brought by events.

==== Stability 穩定度 ====

Stability is one of the crucial components to foster the development of a civilisation, varying from 0 to 100 (by percentage). Stability normally starts from around 60-70 initially

  * It can change overtime, or suddenly decrease by a fixed amount.
  * Can be affected by buffs, events, and religion
  * Different levels of stability has different buffs.

Stability levels:
Stability affects Research speed, Resource extraction, Construction efficiency, Population growth. The percentages below is the effect respectively following the order.
  * 0-10%(Chaotic): -40%, -40%, -40%, -20%
  * 11-20%(Fragile): -30%, -30%, -30%, -15%
  * 21-30%(Precarious): -20%, -20%, -20%, -10%
  * 31-40%(Unsettled): -10%, -10%, -10%, -10%
  * 41-50%(Uneven): 0%, 0%, 0%, -5%
  * 51-60%(Moderate): +5%, +5%, +5%, 0%
  * 61-70%(Steadfast): +10%, +10%, +10%, +5%
  * 71-80%(Robust): +15%, +15%, +15%, +10%
  * 81-90%(Cohesive): +20%, +20%, +20%, +15%
  * 91-100%(Peaceful): +25%, +25%, +25%, +20%
