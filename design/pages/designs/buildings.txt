===== Buildings 建築 =====

Buildings is what the player can construct on a tile.

Buildings are mainly separated to 3 types: Academic, Production, Infrastructure

附属建筑需建造于区域建筑当中。区域建筑升级，附属建筑不会消失。建筑的两格范围内不会再有迷雾。

所有建筑必须且只能由建造者单位建造。每个建筑同时只能由一个建造者建造。

只有住房设施（营地-村落-市镇-城邦）和军事建筑具有生命值。住房设施也是游戏中决定胜负的关键，一旦住房设施生命值清零将会被夺取并成为敌方建筑。

模版释义：

  * 名称：建筑名称
  * 分类：建筑类型
  * 前置：建造所需前置建筑/解锁所需节点
  * 描述：建筑描述
  * 效果：建筑效果（以“，”表示）/可执行操作（以“；”表示）
  * 晋级：可选升级路径
  * 升级条件：升级所需达成的条件
  * 升级消耗：升级所需资源&时间
  * 建造消耗：建造所需资源
  * 建造时间：建造所需时间

  * Research institute
    * Creates the [[research_institutes|Research Institutes 研究院]] desisions
    * The "Capital" of the progression of science
    * The player can modify the general research aim of the institute
  * Laboratory
    * Place for the researcher to do advanced researches
    * Can be upgraded to different types of laboratory: Increase research speed of their respective areas (More specific more buffs)
      * Basic lab (lv.1)
      * Physics Lab (lv.2)
      * Chemistry Lab (lv.2)
      * Special Labs (lv.3 or higher) (e.g. Nuclear Lab)
  * Archive/Library
    * Stores books, manuscripts, and research records
    * Increases research efficiency of all nearby (5 tiles) academic buildings
    * Upgrades (lv. 1-3): 
      * Reading room → Manuscript archive → National depository
  * School
    * Train [[researchers]], Find [[specialists]]
    * Supply Ideas
  * Field station
    * Remote research outpost for specific environments
    * Types: Marine station (coastal), Mountain station (high altitude), Desert station, Forest station
    * Enables region-specific research that cannot be done in main campuses

营地生命值：1000
  * Workshop

  * Processes raw materials into refined goods
  * Types: Smelter (metals), Sawmill (lumber), Textile workshop etc.
  * Can produce specialised equipment for laboratories (glassware, precision tools)
  * Chemical Plants
  * Produce chemicals from raw materials

* Road

  * Connects buildings and enables transport
  * Reduces travel time for researchers (small efficiency buff)
  * Required for resource transportation
  * Upgrades can be unlocked through tech research
  * Bridge
  * Crosses rivers or valleys to connect separated regions
  * Enables access to otherwise unreachable resource sites or field station locations
  * Warehouse/Storage
  * Stores resources
  * Larger warehouses allow stockpiling for times of shortage
  * Power plant
  * Provides energy for advanced laboratories and machinery
  * Progression: Water mill → Steam engine → Coal power plant → Hydroelectric → (future techs)
  * Location requirements: water mills need rivers, windmills need open terrain
