====== Events ======

Events would happen throughout the game, which players are going to make decisions to continue the event, lastly may give bonuses or punishments regarding what they chose.

The events are mostly based on real world past events, with a bit of adjustments. 事件发生时时间会暂停，直到确认后才继续。

按持续事件与瞬时事件划分。

<note tip>MVP只存在瞬时事件</note>

====== Event template ======

通用模版释义：^ Name ^ ID ^ Category ^ No. of sub-events ^ Duration ^ Rareness ^ List of choices ^ List of effects ^ Trigger condition ^

  * Name：事件名称
  * ID：开发者用的编号
  * 描述：事件描述
  * Effects：事件效果
