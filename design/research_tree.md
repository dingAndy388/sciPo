## Research Tree

Discover different science theories, which is crucial to develop a
civilisation.

- Branch:
  - Research [Math](Math), [Physics](Physics), [Chemistry](Chemistry)
    Theories

Researching a topic would require different [resources](resources),
including:

- [Ideas](resources)

Each research topic requires certain amount of time to complete, the
tree progresses down as topics are discovered, unlocking more available
research topics.

初始设定：每棵科技树同时只能进行一项研发。三个科技树互相独立（即最多可以同时进行三个研发）。后续会推出节点使得一棵树可以同时进行多个研发。
