@startuml

UI->>App: BuildRequest(BuildingID, Coord)
App->>App: CheckBasicValidation()

App->>Domain: Build(BuildingID, Coord)

Domain->>Res: IsConsumable()

Domain->>Res: CheckRequirements()
Note over Domain: Traverse IRequirement/IConsumable

alt IsConsumable() AND CheckRequirements()
    Domain->>Res: ResourcesModifier(Resource, Amount)
    Domain->>Map: SaveNewOccupant(BuildingEntity)
    Map ->> Repo: SaveMap()
    Domain-->>UI: BuildSuccess(Event)
else !IsConsumable() OR !CheckRequirements()
    Domain-->>UI: BuildFailed(ErrorMsg)
end
@enduml
